# Viking Game (working title: *Sons of the North*)

A roguelite deckbuilder about three generations of viking raiders. You outfit a
ship, sail out on card-driven raids, haul loot home (if it doesn't drag you to
the bottom of the sea first), grow your settlement, father heirs, and continue
the saga as your sons. Conquering historical locations unlocks permanent boons
for future sagas.
